# Spider-Man Miles Morales artwork

The 5 wallpapers are art inspired by Spider-Man: Miles Morales and Spider-Verse, shared here as fan content for
personal desktops, free of charge. Each picture belongs to its artist, and the work it
shows belongs to Marvel, Sony and Insomniac Games. This theme is not affiliated with or endorsed by any of them.

- `01-into-the-city.jpg`: Miles walking into the city at night; artist unknown
- `02-queens-lights.jpg`: Miles swinging through the Queens lights; artist unknown
- `03-peace-with-darkness.jpg`: Miles, at peace with darkness; artist unknown
- `04-no-mercy.jpg`: Miles, no mercy; artist unknown
- `05-hood.jpg`: Miles' hood; artist unknown

If one of these is yours, open an issue to be credited by name or to have it removed.

The palette is drawn from these pictures: black background, white text, Miles' red accent, red, venom green, gold, blue, glitch magenta and glitch cyan.
